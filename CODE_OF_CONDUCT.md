# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss code and evidence, not someone's identity or
experience level. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and disclosure of private information
are not acceptable. Welcome questions and acknowledge useful contributions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when needed. Reports should
be made through an existing private channel with the maintainer; do not expose personal information
in a public issue. A dedicated private reporting route must be configured before public launch.
